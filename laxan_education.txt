# 🧠 Best free storage options for app image uploads

## 🚀 1. Developer-friendly (best choice)

These are made specifically for apps:

* **Cloudflare R2**

  * ✅ ~10 GB free storage
  * ✅ **Zero egress fees** (huge advantage for apps)
  * ✅ S3-compatible API
  * 👉 Best for: production apps, scaling later
    ([AgentDeals][1])

* **Cloudinary**

  * ✅ Free tier with image transformations (resize, compress, optimize)
  * ✅ Built-in CDN + URL-based transformations
  * 👉 Best for: image-heavy apps (profiles, galleries)
    ([AgentDeals][1])

* **ImageKit**

  * ✅ Free bandwidth + real-time optimization
  * ✅ CDN + URL transformations
  * 👉 Best for: fast image delivery globally
    ([AgentDeals][1])

## ☁️ 2. General cloud storage (simple but limited for apps)

* **Google Drive**

  * ✅ 15 GB free
  * ❌ Not ideal for public image hosting/CDN
    ([TechYorker][2])

* **MEGA**

  * ✅ 20 GB free
  * ❌ No native CDN / not optimized for app serving
    ([Cloudwards][3])

* **pCloud**

  * ✅ ~10 GB free
  * ❌ More for personal storage than backend usage
    ([Cloudwards][3])

👉 Use these only for prototypes or internal tools.

## 💡 What most developers actually do

From real-world dev discussions:

> “For public image links + app usage → Cloudinary or Cloudflare Images are better suited.” ([Reddit][4])

That’s because:

* You get **direct image URLs**
* Built-in **optimization (resize, compress)**

* **Best overall (free + scalable):** → Cloudflare R2

# ⚠️ Important (most people miss this)

Free tiers usually have:

* bandwidth limits
* request limits
* or no CDN

👉 If your app grows, you’ll need to upgrade or switch.
